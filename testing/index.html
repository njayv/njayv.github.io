About Me

Hey! I'm Niall, I'm 17 and I aspire to be (hopfully) a Game Developer, Software Developer or anything down that street. If it involves IT and Creativity, count me in! Currently, I am studying a Level 3 Diploma in Games Design in college, and will be moving onto University, shortly, studying the exact same thing. I've always had an alltime love for video games new and old and always had the thought of looking into making my own one, and one day I did, and it changed everything...

My whole outlook on video games totally changed. Instead of just looking at a game and playing it while thinking "Oh cool I can do this", I think about all of the different ways that a game developer can introduce, implement and polish a certain aspect, ability or even an animation within a video game. Take Fortnite for example, I used to play Fortnite on my Xbox One back when Battle Royale first came out, and didn't have any idea on how difficult and in-depth video game development was. Now that I own a (sub)decent PC, I have always looked into games too much to the point where I've even gone to the point of attempting to remake the controls of a game. But enough of that nonsense, more about myself now.

Currently, my "Skill Set" of sort includes:

  • Game Development - Higher Beginner/Lower Intermediate
  • 3D Modelling - Beginner
  • Texturing/Rendering - Intermediate
  • Animation - Beginner
  • Composing - Intermediate
